# The Lantern Vault — Table Playtest Packet v0.3

**Original fictional adventure, level 1, 3–5 players, 60–90 minutes.** Not for sale or publication. This packet accompanies `the-lantern-vault-playtest-draft.md` and does not replace a finished adventure PDF.

## Dungeon Master preparation
Read the three scenes before play. Set out three ordinary tokens to represent the rotating mirrors, a pencil, and this observation sheet. Do not require a specific class, spell, or single successful roll. Use normal D&D ability-check terminology where relevant; the checks in the draft are optional, not gates.

## Player-facing premise
*Bellmere's guiding lantern has gone dark. The abandoned observatory above the village has begun to chime again. The lamplighter asks for help before nightfall.*

## Scene checklist
- [ ] The Darkened Square: players learn where the apprentice went
- [ ] The Starward Steps: players choose a safe approach
- [ ] The Mirror Room: players solve the mirror/latch problem or negotiate a solution
- [ ] Resolution: players restore the light and understand the apprentice's motive
