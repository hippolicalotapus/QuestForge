# The Lantern Vault — Dungeon Master playtest draft v0.2

**Original adventure concept; level 1, 3–5 players, estimated 60–90 minutes.** Not a complete or sale-ready adventure. All names and events here are fictional.

## Hook: The Darkened Square (15 minutes)
In the village of Bellmere, a lighthouse-like street lantern that guides travelers through evening fog has gone dark. The lamplighter, Mira, reports hearing a metallic chime from the abandoned Starward Observatory. Read aloud: *“At sunset, every window catches a pale glimmer—except the lantern above the square. Beyond the houses, an old dome reflects one lonely star.”*

The characters may investigate soot prints near the lantern or ask Mira's former apprentice, Tavin, about the observatory. Both paths reveal that Tavin went to the dome to repair a mirror array. **Do not gate the adventure behind a single skill check.** A successful DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) check reveals an extra clue: soot contains tiny mirror fragments. Failure still reveals the observatory route.

## The Starward Steps (15–20 minutes)
A cracked staircase winds to the dome. Describe the broken railing before players choose how to proceed. A secured rope, careful teamwork, or the maintenance passage all succeed. A character rushing alone may attempt a DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check; on failure, the character slips onto a lower landing, loses time and suffers no damage. The challenge is navigation and teamwork, not a lethal hazard.

## The Mirror Room (20–30 minutes)
Inside the dome, Tavin is frightened and trapped behind a sliding brass screen. Three pivoting mirrors must point toward the central lens. Give three clear clues: the ceiling depicts a star, the lens bears a star engraving, and the mirrors have matching marks. Characters can align the mirrors through investigation, work with Tavin's spoken directions, or release the manual latch. Every route can resolve the scene. Do not require a particular spell, character class or skill proficiency.

## Resolution (10 minutes)
When the lantern reignites, Tavin apologizes for trying to repair it alone. Mira offers the characters thanks and a symbolic brass star token. The village's travel routes become safer. Ask the group which clues were clear and which felt confusing.
