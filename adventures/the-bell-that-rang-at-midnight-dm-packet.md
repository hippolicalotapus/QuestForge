# The Bell That Rang at Midnight — Dungeon Master playtest packet v0.2

**PRIVATE PLAYTEST ONLY · NOT FOR PUBLICATION OR SALE**  
**Level:** 1 · **Players:** 3–5 · **Target:** 45–60 minutes · **Format:** free introductory episode to *The Lantern Vault*

## Quick start (5 minutes before play)
Read `the-bell-that-rang-at-midnight.md`. Prepare the three scene zones below, three paper tokens (bell, pivoting shard, brass star), and the player handouts. Agree on content boundaries. No mandatory class, spell, combat encounter, or ability-check success is needed.

### Scene clocks and fallback prompts
| Clock | Scene | Player objective | If running long |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–12 min | Bellmere square | Discover why the bell rings and the guiding lantern went dark | Mira points out the route and the silvery grit |
| 12–25 min | Tower approach | Enter safely using a chosen method | Show the maintenance entrance clearly |
| 25–45 min | Bell chamber | Stop the mechanical striker; solve through at least one of three methods | Show the beam, pivot and faded diagram without requiring a roll |
| 45–60 min | Return to square | Restore the light and receive the star-token mystery | Shorten optional traveler scene; preserve resolution |

**Fast group:** Add the lost traveler and let players trace the shard's beam. **Slow group:** Remove optional checks and give all three chamber clues. Do not cut the free episode's resolved bell-and-lantern ending.

## Player handout 1 — Mira's request
> The abandoned tower rang at midnight. The guiding lantern is out, and fog is swallowing the road. Please quiet the bell and help us bring back the light. — Mira, Bellmere lamplighter

## Player handout 2 — faded chamber diagram
> STARWARD MAINTENANCE — When the light strikes the turning glass, secure the striker at rest. Never force the bell while the beam is aligned.

Give handout 2 only when the party inspects the diagram; do not make reading it the only solution.

## Player handout 3 — star-token discovery
> A brass star. Its reverse bears a small engraved route from Bellmere to the Starward Observatory. Three tiny marks sit beside the dome.

Give handout 3 after the immediate crisis is solved. The observatory clue is a **new question**, not an unpaid ending.

## Seamless paid-adventure transfer
Record three items: **(1)** the party knows Mira, **(2)** the bell is silent and the square lantern was relit, **(3)** the party has the brass star and knows Tavin is missing. If players continue into *The Lantern Vault*, **skip the paid adventure's initial Darkened Square crisis**; instead, Mira reports that the lantern dims again at the observatory's three chimes, a distinct new disturbance, and points to Tavin's route. Proceed to the **Starward Steps**. Do not make players repeat the bell-chamber puzzle or award a second brass star. If starting *The Lantern Vault* without this free episode, use its existing Darkened Square opening unchanged.
