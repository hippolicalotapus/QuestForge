# The Bell That Rang at Midnight — QuestForge free introduction v0.1

**Private playtest draft.** Level 1, 3–5 characters; **45–60 minutes**. A free introductory episode to *The Lantern Vault*. This is not published, sale-ready, or a substitute for a finished professionally illustrated adventure. All characters, locations and story elements are original fictional concepts.

## Promise to players
The immediate danger at the bell tower has a satisfying resolution. The wider mystery remains open, leading into the **paid, complete** *Lantern Vault* adventure. Never require a purchase to finish the free episode's immediate objective.

## Dungeon Master preparation (5 minutes)
Prepare a rough reference sketch (not a release-quality map) of Bellmere square, a short stairway, and a bell chamber with two exits. Use three tokens: one for the bell, one for the rope, one for a brass star. Have the four scene beats below ready. No mandatory spell, class, ability check, combat, or paid resource. Use the group's agreed content boundaries; no graphic harm. The village's guiding lantern and the abandoned Starward Observatory remain consistent with *The Lantern Vault*.

## Scene 1 — The midnight chime (0–12 minutes)
Read aloud: *“Midnight passes. The abandoned bell tower rings once. Every lamp in Bellmere flickers; the guiding lantern in the square goes dark. Mira, the lamplighter, looks toward the tower. ‘That bell has had no rope for twenty years.’”*

Mira asks the characters to stop the bell before the fog obscures the road. The characters can question Mira, examine the square, or follow the sound. All approaches reveal the tower route. An optional DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) check spots silvery grit by the lantern; failure does not conceal the route. Mira recalls that her apprentice Tavin went to the observatory earlier.

## Scene 2 — The tower approach (12–25 minutes)
The lower stair is loose and the main door is jammed. Players may wedge the door, use the rear maintenance entrance, or stabilize the stair. No roll is necessary for a sensible plan. An optional DC 10 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check may resolve a hurried attempt; on failure, a loose step causes noise and lost time, **not damage or a blocked path**. A bell note sounds when someone touches a brass star on the door.

## Scene 3 — The bell chamber (25–45 minutes)
A loose brass striker swings whenever a narrow beam from the observatory crosses a polished shard in the bell chamber. The ringing is mechanical, not a creature. Three clues support multiple solutions: the bell rings only when the light flashes; the shard pivots on a pin; a faded diagram shows the striker's resting position. Characters can turn the shard away, secure the striker, shade the beam, or improvise another plausible fix. The chamber quiets. **No single failed check can stall the episode.**

Optional tension: the fog reaches the lower steps, requiring the party to choose whether to secure the door or guide a lost traveler outside. Both choices can succeed through teamwork; avoid adding an unbalanced level-one combat encounter merely to fill time.

## Scene 4 — A promise and a question (45–60 minutes)
Mira relights the square's guiding lantern using the recovered brass star. The road is safe again, and the bell remains silent: **the free episode's problem is solved**. Mira gives the characters the brass star token as a keepsake.

As they leave, the star warms in their hands. The Starward Observatory answers with three faint chimes. On the token's reverse is a small engraved map pointing to the observatory. Mira says, *“Tavin was working there. He should have come home hours ago.”*

**Continuation:** Players who choose the paid *Lantern Vault* begin at its Darkened Square hook with the star token and their knowledge of Mira and Tavin. Skip repeated introductions. The free episode is still a complete short session if they stop here.

## Player handout (draft text)
*Bellmere's bell tower has been silent for twenty years. Tonight it rings, and the lantern guiding travelers through the fog goes dark. Mira the lamplighter asks you to restore the light before anyone is lost.*

## Dungeon Master pacing and observations
If the group moves quickly, let players investigate the shard diagram and speak with the traveler. If behind schedule, Mira provides the rear-entrance clue and the bell's three clues immediately. Record actual run time, clarity of the three clues, whether choices mattered, and whether players wanted to continue.
