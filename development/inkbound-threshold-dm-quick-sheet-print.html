Location keys (DM map only)

Fail-forward and safety

Never hide the seed pouch behind a required roll. Careful movement avoids the root hazard. The keeper speaks before combat and stops if the journal remains unharmed. No automatic hazard damage. Read clues aloud or provide large print. Respect player boundaries.
