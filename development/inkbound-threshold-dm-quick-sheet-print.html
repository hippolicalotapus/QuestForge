Location keys (DM map only)

1 Gate: seed pouch and three emblems; alternate latch. 2 Hall: visible roots, trough, watering can, brass leaf token. 3 Cellar: keeper and journal. 4 Square: fountain with five-petal emblem and compartment.

Fail-forward and safety

Never hide the seed pouch behind a required roll. A failed DC 12 latch attempt opens on a noisy second attempt after one extra minute. Careful movement avoids the root hazard; a failed rushing save causes prone, with no automatic hazard damage. The keeper speaks before combat: returning the leaf token, succeeding at DC 11 Persuasion, or protecting the journal after failed Persuasion allows recovery without combat. Keep the token instead for advantage on one botanical-clue check. A full scarecrow stat block is not appropriate for this level-1 encounter; combat is optional and any stand-in requires separate balance and rights review. Read clues aloud or provide large print. Respect player boundaries.

Reward and pacing

Mara pays 20 gp total on journal return whether the fountain is visited before or afterward. The fountain is optional; reveal the first-frost note only if explored. For a 60-minute session: hook 8, gate 10, hall 10, keeper 15, resolution 10, feedback 7 minutes. For 90 minutes: use the 80-minute table plus 10 minutes for exploration or breaks.
