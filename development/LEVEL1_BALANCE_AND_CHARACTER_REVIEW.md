### Concrete reconciliation blockers

1. Each sheet gives a descriptive background/story role, but does not identify its mechanical background and Origin feat choices. Record those existing choices before treating the builds as complete 2024-style pregens.
2. The three Human sheets list Heroic Inspiration but do not identify the Human Versatile Origin feat choice. Preserve existing characters and recover or explicitly choose the missing build options in a subsequent character-production pass; do not invent what was previously approved.
3. Reconcile each story hook with the selected adventure while retaining the original Scholar's Last Message sheets unchanged as source material.
4. Review all class/species features, spell components, prepared choices, equipment and printable usability against one recorded rules edition. For example, verify how Lyra's quarterstaff serves as or is supplemented by the required spellcasting equipment rather than inferring it from an equipment label.
5. Bring the reconciled, reviewed source sheets into the private development package and validate one page per character before release readiness is marked complete.
