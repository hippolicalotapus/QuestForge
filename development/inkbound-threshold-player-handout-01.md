# The Inkbound Threshold — Player Handout 01
**Manymorrow Studios / The Voynich Codex — Level 1 private playtest material**

## Archivist Mara Fen's request
> The rain has reached the scriptorium's cellar. Please bring back my missing field journal before the ink is lost. The old gardener marked his locks with plants rather than keys. I can pay twenty gold pieces when you return.

## The greenhouse plate
> ONLY WHAT GROWS MAY PASS.

Three carved emblems stand above the gate: **SEED · FLAME · KEY**.

## The recovered journal leaf
The leaf shows a flower with **five petals** and a drawing of the village square. Below it, the gardener has written:

> **P E T A L S   P O I N T   T O   W A T E R**

The remaining ink is faded, but the five-petaled flower and the words above are clear.

### For players
This is an in-world handout. The greenhouse plate is given at the gate; the recovered journal leaf is given only after the journal is found. Do not read the journal leaf early.
