# The Inkbound Threshold — Dungeon Master playtest packet
**Private development draft · Level 1 · 4 characters · 60–90 minutes · not for sale or publication**

## Running order and time budget
| Segment | Target | Core purpose | Fail-forward option |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Mara Fen's request | 10 min | Set urgency and reward | Mara gives the gardener's seed clue |
| Broken gate | 15 min | Explore and choose an approach | Find the seed pouch or open side latch |
| Rootbound hall | 15 min | Telegraph hazard and reward observation | Cross slowly without a roll |
| Cellar keeper | 20 min | Negotiate, return token or face optional conflict | Keeper stops if journal remains unharmed |
| Fountain resolution | 10 min | Resolve the journal clue and seed next adventure | Mara identifies the five-petal emblem |
| Debrief | 10 min | Capture friction and accessibility notes | Record feedback privately |

## DM quick reference
**Opening:** Mara offers **20 gp total to the party**, not per character. Rainwater threatens the journal. The journal is not a reproduction or translation of a real historical manuscript.

**Gate:** Three emblems: seed, flame, key. Plant the available seed to open the gate in one minute. DC 10 Investigation to infer the solution; DC 12 Athletics to force the side latch. On a failed latch check, a noisy second attempt opens it after one additional minute; the roll changes time and noise, not whether the party can proceed. Never require a successful roll to discover the seed pouch.

**Hall:** Characters can notice the roots before crossing. A character rushing in makes DC 11 Dexterity save or falls prone; **no automatic damage**. Watering the dry trough clears roots and reveals a brass leaf token. Cautious half-speed travel is safe.

**Keeper:** Begin with conversation, not automatic initiative. The guardian accepts the leaf token or DC 11 Persuasion after hearing the request. If players initiate combat, use a legally sourced and separately reviewed level-appropriate creature stat block; the provisional crawling-claw stand-in has **not** been balanced or rights-checked for publication. Do not reproduce any third-party stat block in this packet. Guardian stops fighting if journal is unharmed.

**Journal and reward:** The five-petaled flower is a botanical clue, **not a cipher**. “PETALS POINT TO WATER” refers to the village fountain. Investigation DC 10 helps; players can infer the answer without rolling. Mara pays **20 gp total** when the journal is returned, whether the party visits the fountain before or after receiving payment. If they return first, Mara identifies the five-petal fountain emblem and offers the fountain visit as an optional epilogue. Do not gate the reward or the lead behind a successful roll. The fountain compartment contains an empty vial and “The spring wakes at first frost.”

## Ready-to-run keeper conversation (private DM material)
When the characters enter the cellar, the keeper steps between them and the journal. Read aloud: *A straw-stuffed figure straightens in the damp gloom. Its hands tremble around the book. “Are you here to take the last thing that still grows?”*

**First exchange:** Ask the players what they want. If they mention Mara, the keeper asks, *“Will she keep its pages dry?”* An honest promise to protect the journal opens the DC 11 Persuasion approach; do not require a roll if the players present the brass leaf token and offer it back. Returning the token is a choice: the players may instead keep it for advantage on one later botanical-clue interpretation check, as described in the adventure draft. Neither choice is mandatory to progress.

**If the players fail Persuasion:** The keeper does not attack automatically. It demands that the party wrap the journal against the rain using cloth, a cloak, or another plausible covering. Meeting that request earns its trust without a second roll. If the players cannot provide a covering, they may offer to carry the book carefully and show how; accept a credible alternative. Failure costs conversation time, not access to the journal.

**If the players threaten the keeper:** Warn them clearly that it is frightened rather than aggressive. It retreats behind the workbench and offers one last chance to talk. If players insist on combat, pause to use only a separately verified legal, level-appropriate combat reference; this packet does not certify a balanced encounter. The keeper disengages or yields when the journal is protected. Never require defeating it to obtain the book.

**If the group runs short of time:** After two exchanges, the keeper asks for a simple promise to keep the journal dry, then steps aside. Move directly to the journal clue. Do not reveal the fountain compartment's first-frost note before the party reaches the fountain.

## Four-path rehearsal for the first private playtest
Use these dry-run paths before inviting players. They are facilitator rehearsals, not evidence of a completed player playtest.

| Path | Player choices | DM response | Required outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — Puzzle solvers | Plant seed; water roots; return leaf token; visit fountain before Mara | Open gate after one minute; clear hall; keeper accepts token; reveal fountain note only at fountain | Journal recovered, fountain clue earned, Mara pays 20 gp when journal returned |
| B — Failed rolls | Fail gate Athletics; rush roots and fail Dexterity; fail keeper Persuasion | Side latch yields after noisy extra minute; character falls prone without automatic damage; keeper accepts protective wrapping | No dead end, no forced combat, journal returned for 20 gp |
| C — Careful negotiators | Search for pouch; cross hall at half speed; water the trough to reveal the token; keep token; promise to protect journal; return to Mara first | Supply note and seed; safe crossing; reveal token only after watering; keeper permits no-roll protective plan; Mara pays and identifies fountain | Optional fountain epilogue remains available after payment |
| D — Time-limited group | Miss initial seed clue; skip trough; refuse combat; need to end by 60 minutes | Reveal gardener note; permit careful crossing; keeper steps aside after promise; Mara points out emblem | Journal and 20 gp resolved; fountain hook can close with an invitation, not a forced extra scene |

**Rehearsal pass criteria:** Every path reaches journal recovery without a mandatory successful die roll, avoids automatic combat, preserves the player's choice about the leaf token, and never reveals the first-frost message before the fountain. Record any mismatch as a manuscript or handout defect before inviting players. These paths do not establish encounter balance, print quality, or real-user usability.

## Timing and table-size controls
For a **60-minute session**, aim for 8 minutes on the hook, 10 at the gate, 10 in the hall, 15 with the keeper, 10 for return and optional fountain resolution, and 7 for feedback. For a **90-minute session**, use the detailed running-order table above (80 minutes) and allow 10 minutes of flexible exploration or breaks. If the fountain is not visited, Mara can identify its emblem as a future lead; do not narrate the sealed compartment's contents until players actually investigate it. If the party separates, rotate spotlight turns and offer the next clue to whichever character has not yet contributed; do not require a particular character class or skill proficiency.

## Location key for professional maps
1. Gate: three emblems, seed pouch, gardener's note, alternate side latch.
2. Rootbound hall: visible roots, dry trough, watering can, brass leaf token.
3. Cellar: keeper, journal, exit.
4. Village square: five-petal fountain emblem and concealed compartment.

**Player map:** show accessible paths, gate, hall, cellar and square, but no hidden token or compartment. **DM map:** numbered key, alternative entrance and clue positions. Both maps must be original, legible at print size, and separately reviewed.

## Private playtest setup and handout timing
Before the session, print the player map and two separate player handout sheets. Keep the DM map, this packet, and the DM quick sheet behind the screen. Give the gate sheet at Scene 1 and the recovered journal sheet only after Scene 3. Do not hand out the combined source Markdown file, which contains both clues.

For a first run, record actual elapsed time for each scene and whether the group used the seed, side latch, cautious crossing, or negotiation options. Pause to check that the fountain clue can be solved without a required die roll. Collect player and DM feedback separately on paper until the separate online forms and QR codes are tested. Do not record player names or contact details in the public repository.

## Session safety and accessibility
At the start, invite players to name content boundaries privately or aloud without pressure. Avoid graphic descriptions of injury; the only mandatory hazard consequence is prone. Provide printed large-text clue text or read it aloud. Allow alternative verbal, observational, or creative approaches without punishing players for disability-related barriers.

## First-playtest readiness gate
Before inviting players, the Dungeon Master should verify that the four mapped locations match the printed handouts, that the journal leaf remains concealed until recovery, and that the optional combat reference is legal and suitable for four Level 1 characters. If that combat reference has not been reviewed, use negotiation and surrender as the default and do not present the encounter as balance-tested. Keep the session private and do not share the DM map or source handout with players.

## Map and encounter table-readiness checklist (private)
Before printing, verify both map titles identify their intended audience and that the gate, hall, cellar, and fountain remain in matching positions. The three ceramic pots must be visible at the gate; the broken-glasshouse roof detail must not conceal labels or suggest an inaccessible doorway. Check the map at **100% print size**, not only on a phone screen. Keep the DM-only seed pouch, token, journal and compartment annotations out of the player map.

**Dry-run the four routes without dice:** seed planted; noisy side latch after failure; careful crossing with no fall; keeper accepts a journal-protection promise after failed Persuasion. Then rehearse a rushing character failing the DC 11 Dexterity save: they fall prone but take no automatic damage. The party still recovers the journal and earns **20 gp total** regardless of fountain order. Track time and spotlight opportunities for all four characters; flag any mandatory roll or combat as a defect.

**Optional combat gate:** do not substitute the full scarecrow monster. If the DM has not reviewed a legal, level-appropriate single-creature reference and its risks, skip combat and use the keeper's negotiation or retreat branches. Do not mark combat balance complete until actual supervised level-1 testing supports it.
