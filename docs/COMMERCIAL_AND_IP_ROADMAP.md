# QuestForge: Commercial and IP Development Plan
Status: approved strategic direction; planning document, not a claim of completed functionality or legal clearance.

## Product priorities
1. PartyForge character import (PDF, photo, and structured exports) with review and provenance.
2. Party analysis, including mixed-level balance, player preferences, and consent-based backstory use.
3. Adventure Creator with original, First Age, homebrew, and user-provided D&D campaign reference modes.
4. Complete DM-ready generation: hooks, scenes, encounters, NPCs, rewards, maps, handouts, print-ready PDFs, validation.
5. Adventure library: free Voynich lead magnet and paid adventures; verify deliverables before activating checkout.

## Publishing priorities
- First Age story bible: canon timeline, Shaping, Concord, War of Glass and Fang, Great Forgetting, Aethrion, Last Witness, character arcs.
- World Compendium: geography, civilizations, planar cosmology, history.
- Primordial Bestiary: independently original creature taxonomy and rules-safe game statistics.
- Grimoire of the First Dawn: original spells and artifacts.
- Atlas of Forgotten Worlds: production-quality maps.
- Adventurer's Guide: playable character options, equipment, adventures.

## IP separation
Independent edition must stand on its own without protected D&D settings, characters, lore, artwork, or trademarks. D&D-connected edition is a contingent licensed adaptation. User-provided campaign context is not a blanket grant to redistribute proprietary content. Do not assume SRD grants official setting or artwork rights. Review current license and attribution before release. Do not send confidential First Age manuscripts or implementation details as an unsolicited pitch.

## Wizards partnership path
Stage A: nonconfidential business licensing inquiry for proper contact, authorized art/logo/map use (website and generated packages), settings, character sheet designs, D&D Beyond integration, interactive generation, and potential content marketplace.
Stage B: demo plus user research and working products; request pilot terms and scope.
Stage C: only after polished story bible and representative bestiary, explore First Age adaptation/publishing. Negotiate royalties, audit, sublicensing, generated output rights, territories, term, termination, approvals, exclusivity, and ownership with legal counsel. No agreement or permission currently exists.

## Revenue hypotheses (not approved prices)
- Free starter adventure and limited creation to demonstrate quality.
- Paid complete adventure exports or generation credits.
- Optional subscriptions for ongoing campaigns and continuity.
- Licensed add-ons/featured products only with rights and a viable revenue-share agreement.
Track per-generation AI cost, payment fees, refunds, tax, conversion, and support cost before choosing pricing.

## Acceptance gates
- Test character import with varied real sheets and manual correction.
- Verify generator outputs are coherent, rules-audited, accessible and usable by a DM.
- Verify generated content doesn't reproduce unlicensed protected source text/art.
- Test PDF printing, map quality, checkout, digital delivery, refunds and privacy.
- Explicit approval before paid services, live deployment, or real customer charges.

## Next implementation milestone
Design typed PartyProfile + AdventureBrief schema and deterministic local generator; build tested end-to-end flow from imported party to editable adventure draft; then connect a properly secured model-backed generation service with cost controls. Existing website prototypes are not yet validated production systems.
