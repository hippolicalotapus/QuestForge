# QuestForge first paid-adventure release gate — November 13, 2026

**Internal pre-launch checklist; no products are for sale.** Target the first original level-one adventure as a downloadable digital product. This document is a release gate, not evidence of a finished PDF or payment integration.

## Product acceptance checks
- Original story, encounters and maps; verify image licensing and originality.
- Professional print-ready adventure PDF, readable on phones and at standard paper sizes.
- Dungeon Master (DM) instructions, level-one balance and encounter testing.
- Separate player and DM feedback QR codes that resolve to **working** endpoints; anonymous submissions and optional contact information; test both codes on a phone.
- Preview pages that do not misrepresent unfinished maps as final.
- Download integrity test, filename, version and changelog.
- Explicit commercial price, taxes, refund/download terms and payment provider suitability verified before sales.
- Accessible file and reasonable file size; no live customer data in source control.
